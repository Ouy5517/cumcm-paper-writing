# Mechanistic-model validation

## Required inputs

Give governing equations, units, initial and boundary conditions, and parameter sources.
For time-dependent motion or transport, distinguish process start from observation
start and map time to position with explicit units. Use the relevant section of
references/temporal-validation.md when this affects model or constraint validity.

## Primary diagnostics

Perform dimensional/numerical checks and assess parameter identifiability.
Do not compare lumped and spatial models solely by their fit to one calibration
record; report what observations identify and what is assumed.

## Validation

Compare predictions with independent observations or known physical limits.

## Sensitivity

Vary identifiable parameters and initial or boundary conditions.

## Failure boundary

State regimes where governing assumptions or parameter sources do not apply.

## Do not claim

Do not claim mechanism proof from fit alone.
