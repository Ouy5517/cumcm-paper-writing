# Mechanistic-model validation

## Required inputs

Give governing equations, units, initial and boundary conditions, and parameter sources.

## Primary diagnostics

Perform dimensional/numerical checks and assess parameter identifiability.

## Validation

Compare predictions with independent observations or known physical limits.

## Sensitivity

Vary identifiable parameters and initial or boundary conditions.

## Failure boundary

State regimes where governing assumptions or parameter sources do not apply.

## Do not claim

Do not claim mechanism proof from fit alone.
