# Simulation-model validation

## Required inputs

Specify state transitions/events, distributions, calibration data, random seed, and replications.

## Primary diagnostics

Report convergence/Monte Carlo error, calibration fit, and replication summaries.

## Validation

Compare simulated outputs with held-out observations or independently known behavior.

## Sensitivity

Vary distributions, calibration inputs, event rules, and random seeds.

## Failure boundary

Identify rare events, uncalibrated regimes, and insufficient replication.

## Do not claim

Do not draw a single-run conclusion or a real-world causal claim.
