# Evaluation-model validation

## Required inputs

State the indicator direction and scaling for every indicator, the weight method,
and any consistency test.

## Primary diagnostics

Show indicator coverage, weight values, and consistency results.

## Validation

Check weight stability and ranking stability under defensible alternative inputs or methods.

## Sensitivity

Vary scaling, indicator inclusion, and weighting assumptions.

## Failure boundary

Identify rankings that change materially when subjective choices change.

## Do not claim

Do not make an objective-best claim from a subjective weighting scheme.
