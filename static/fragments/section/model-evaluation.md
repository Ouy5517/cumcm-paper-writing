## Purpose

Give evidence-based strengths, weaknesses, measured cost, and an applicability boundary.

## Required inputs

Use validation and sensitivity findings, implementation records, assumptions, and the evidence ledger.

## Structure

Connect each strength or weakness to evidence, report measured cost only, and specify the usable scope.

## Evidence boundary

Do not claim runtime, robustness, or superiority without a recorded measurement or comparison.

## Common failures

Generic praise, unmeasured cost claims, or an applicability statement without a boundary.
