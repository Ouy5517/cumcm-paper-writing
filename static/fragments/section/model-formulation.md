## Purpose

Specify the model so its objective or state equations, limits, and selection rationale are reproducible.

## Required inputs

Use the problem-analysis criteria, notation ledger, assumptions, data definitions, and model alternatives.

## Structure

Give objective or state equations, constraints, domains, parameters, and the rationale for model selection.

## Evidence boundary

Interpret every formula and identify estimated, supplied, and unresolved parameters.

## Common failures

Uninterpreted formulas, incomplete domains or constraints, or rationale detached from the problem demand.
