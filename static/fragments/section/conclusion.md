## Purpose

Close the argument by answering every subproblem consistently with the abstract and validation.

## Required inputs

Use the coverage matrix, stabilized results, validation findings, and abstract draft.

## Structure

Answer each subproblem, summarize qualified implications, and state the established boundary.

## Evidence boundary

Introduce no new data, experiments, or claims beyond the supported body and validation.

## Common failures

Adding new evidence, leaving a subproblem unanswered, or contradicting the abstract.
