## Purpose

Make assumptions testable and establish one ledger-backed vocabulary for quantities and symbols.

## Required inputs

Use the evidence ledger, data definitions, model scope, and terminology/notation ledger.

## Structure

For every assumption give its consequence and testability; define symbols, indices, domains, and units once.

## Evidence boundary

Mark unsupported assumptions and avoid treating convenient simplifications as observed facts.

## Common failures

Undefined symbols, changing units, duplicate definitions, or assumptions without consequences.
