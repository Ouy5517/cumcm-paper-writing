## Purpose

Provide a traceable record of sources actually inspected and cited in the manuscript.

## Required inputs

Use in-text citations, source notes, inspected documents, and the evidence ledger.

## Structure

Preserve the in-text mapping and list each inspected source with available bibliographic metadata.

## Evidence boundary

Flag unverifiable metadata rather than fabricating authors, dates, venues, or identifiers.

## Common failures

Listing unread sources, broken in-text mapping, or invented citation metadata.
