## Purpose

Translate each prompt demand into a deliverable, evidence need, and model-choice criterion.

## Required inputs

Use the original prompt, available data, constraints, and the problem-coverage matrix.

## Structure

For each demand, state the required output, evidence source, and criterion for selecting a model.

## Evidence boundary

Distinguish supplied facts from interpretations and identify missing inputs and their consequences.

## Common failures

Mechanically restating the prompt or choosing a model without an output-linked reason.
