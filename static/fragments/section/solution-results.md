## Purpose

Present each subproblem's method and result together so the answer is traceable to its computation.

## Required inputs

Use checked derivations or executable methods, result records, figures or tables,
the coverage matrix, and the evidence ledger.

## Structure

For each subproblem, state the method, result, interpretation, and result type beside one another.
Keep enough decision values and constraint/validation evidence in the body to
answer the question. Put long event schedules or per-item/per-day detail in
traceable result files or appendices as permitted; check the full requested
horizon rather than relying on a representative excerpt's title.

## Evidence boundary

Label values as observed, fitted, predicted, or scenario values; do not merge these categories.

## Common failures

Separating methods from their results, hiding result provenance, or presenting scenarios as observations.
