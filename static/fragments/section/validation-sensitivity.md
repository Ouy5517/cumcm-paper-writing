## Purpose

Establish credibility through model-family-appropriate checks and material sensitivity analysis.

## Required inputs

Use the selected model family, validation design, baseline or feasibility checks, and material assumptions or parameters.

## Structure

Match validation to the model family, vary material inputs, and report changes, instability, and boundaries.

## Evidence boundary

Separate fit from validation and state when external validation or sufficient data are unavailable.

## Common failures

Using one generic metric for every model, varying immaterial inputs, or concealing unstable conclusions.
