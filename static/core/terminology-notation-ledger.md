# Terminology and notation ledger

Create and maintain the ledger before repeated drafting and revise it whenever
the model or evidence changes. Its schema is:

| Canonical form | Definition/first use | Symbol or abbreviation | Unit | Precision | Variants/collisions | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [term] | [definition and first location] | [symbol] | [unit] | [rule] | [source forms] | [adopted form and rationale] |

Use one concept per canonical name and one symbol per quantity within a model.
Give a definition before use, keep stable units, and use one numeric precision
per repeated metric. Record a change rather than silently varying a form.

Ask the author only when a collision is genuinely ambiguous. Otherwise adopt
the dominant source form and record that decision, including the discarded
variant where it could confuse a reader. Preserve canonical technical forms in
Chinese prose, equations, tables, captions, code excerpts, and figures.
