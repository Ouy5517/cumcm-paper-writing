# Year and rule provenance

Resolve the selected year from the author, official notice, or supplied template,
not today's date. An exact requirement becomes a **binding CUMCM rule** only
after its year-specific authority has been inspected. Writing and layout advice
that does not come from that authority is **non-binding editorial guidance**.
For an unknown year, continue evidence-based writing but leave numerical page,
file-size, and disclosure constraints unverified. Do not claim submission readiness.

For each binding CUMCM rule record: rule ID, contest year, document title, issuing
organization, official URL or supplied filename, publication date, section/page,
exact supporting wording, checked date, and verification status. A file hash is
optional provenance, not a prerequisite for verifying an inspected source.
Use VERIFIED only after inspecting that source. A supplied template is a
typography dependency, not proof of an official CUMCM rule; an official notice
outranks conflicting template comments.

The historical summaries in requirements.md have no bundled source snapshots or
complete citations. Treat them as UNVERIFIED research pointers until checked.
Do not merge their limits across years. Preserve provenance in the case report.

For final delivery track format, AI use/disclosure, registration/submission
notice and applicable regional supplements separately as VERIFIED / UNVERIFIED /
NOT APPLICABLE. One format PDF does not establish complete rule coverage.
Check separate deadlines and file rules at the point they affect delivery.
Keep team review evidence factual; AI-generated checks do not establish that
participants led and reviewed the work. Missing rules block readiness, not
independent mathematical work or a clearly labeled draft build.
