# Year and rule provenance

Resolve the contest year from the author or supplied notice, not today's date.
For an unknown year, continue evidence-based writing but leave numerical page,
file-size, and disclosure constraints unverified. Do not claim submission readiness.

For each binding rule record: rule ID, contest year, document title, issuing
organization, official URL or supplied filename and SHA256, publication date,
section/page, exact supporting wording, checked date, and verification status.
Use VERIFIED only after inspecting that source. A template is a typography
dependency, not proof of an official competition rule.

The historical summaries in requirements.md have no bundled source snapshots or
complete citations. Treat them as UNVERIFIED reference notes until checked.
Do not merge their limits across years. Preserve provenance in the case report.
