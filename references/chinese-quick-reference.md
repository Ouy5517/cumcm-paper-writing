# 全国大学生数学建模竞赛论文格式规范（2026年修订稿）

This quick reference preserves exact labels and declarations for Chinese
generation. The detailed baseline is in references/requirements.md; a newer
official notice or division rule overrides it.

## 常用页面与交付标签

- 承诺书：physical page 1 only; do not put it in the electronic paper.
- 编号专用页：physical page 2 only; do not put it in the electronic paper.
- 摘要专用页：the electronic paper starts here and numbers this page as 1.
- 正文：the 2026 baseline starts it on physical page 4.
- 附录：include the file list and complete runnable code.
- 参考文献：cite sources at the point of use and list them consistently.
- 匿名：anonymous sections cannot reveal contestants, schools, or divisions.
- 纸质版 / 电子版：keep their page order and exclusions separate.
- 附件 / 支撑材料：describe the archive contents in the appendix.

- 源程序：include every complete runnable source program in the appendix, or use the exact no-program declaration.

## Exact declarations

- No program used: “本论文没有用到程序”.
- No supporting material: “本论文没有支撑材料”.

## 2026 baseline wording

- A4 margins: each side is at least 2.5 cm (厘米).
- Body: “不超过30页”; 正文从第四页开始.
- Electronic paper and support archive: “文件大小不超过20MB”.
- 不要目录。页码从摘要页开始用阿拉伯数字 1，页脚居中。

Use these Chinese strings exactly when the official form requires them. Do not
translate, shorten, or replace the two declarations with a paraphrase.
