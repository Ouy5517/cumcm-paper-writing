# Consistency sweep

Load this reference only for a full-paper or multi-round consistency review, or
when abstract/body/conclusion disagreement is suspected. Treat the sweep as a
mechanical review before editorial rewriting.

## Sweep order

1. **Count variants** of terms, symbols, units, metric names, numbers, labels,
   and cross-reference targets with search tools first.
2. **Inspect context** for every candidate variant: determine whether it is a
   true inconsistency, a scoped distinction, a quotation, or an ambiguous form.
3. Compare abstract/body/conclusion numbers, terminology, notation, units,
   numeric precision, equation/figure/table/reference cross-references, model
   and metric names, and claims against their tables/figures.
4. Remove prose that merely copies display values; retain interpretation,
   comparisons, assumptions, and limitations.
5. Apply only unambiguous corrections. Do not auto-correct ambiguous variants;
   flag them for author resolution with their contexts.
6. Repeat until no new material inconsistency appears, then rebuild dependent
   artifacts such as the PDF, figures, tables, appendices, and
   submission package.

## Check record

For each changed item, note canonical form, locations checked, rationale, and
dependent artifact. Recheck the source display and the prose claim together so
a visually correct table cannot support a stale conclusion. Stop only after a
full repeat finds no new material inconsistency.
