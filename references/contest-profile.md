# Contest profile

Create case-local contest-profile.yaml. Required fields: actual contest name,
division, year (or null), language, source authority, front matter, page rules,
identity policy, appendix policy, support-file rules, AI disclosure and template.
For authority record document title, URL or supplied file/hash, date, clause/page,
checked date and VERIFIED/UNVERIFIED status. Unknown obligations remain unresolved.

Page rules must identify counted components: summary, body, references, appendix,
disclosures. Do not assume A4, summary-only page 1, anonymous delivery, no contents,
fixed page/file limits or complete-code appendix for all contests.

For CUMCM, year-selection.md and years/ contain historical research pointers only.
For MCM/ICM inspect the selected year's organizer and problem instructions,
including requested letters or other deliverables. For courses follow the rubric.
User draft preferences are conventions, not official rules. Report conflicts.
Templates supply typography only within verified requirements.
